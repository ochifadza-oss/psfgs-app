The Problem: Governance Failures Are Systemic and Recurring

South African municipalities face a compounding governance crisis. Despite billions invested in audit support, consultants, and training, the number of municipalities with clean audits has stagnated below 30 for six consecutive years. The root cause is not a lack of awareness — it is a lack of structured, real-time tooling to track, remediate, and evidence corrective action between audits.

Accounting Officers, CFOs, and Directors operate across disconnected spreadsheets, email chains, and manual registers. Findings raised in October are forgotten by March. Evidence submitted to Internal Audit is lost or unverifiable. Section 71 reports are compiled at month-end in a panic. Consequence management cases stall for years without tracking. The AGSA raises the same findings. Audit opinions deteriorate.

The cost of non-compliance: A municipality with a Qualified opinion forfeits credibility with investors, development finance institutions, and the National Treasury — triggering Section 139 interventions, withheld equitable share allocations, and reputational damage that affects service delivery for millions of residents.

The Solution: PSFGS — Governance Infrastructure for Every Municipality

The Public Sector Financial Governance Suite (PSFGS) is a South African-built, MFMA-native, cloud-based platform that digitises and automates the full spectrum of municipal financial governance — from the moment an audit finding is raised to the day it is formally closed.

How It Works

  • Cloud-hosted SaaS — no hardware procurement required
  • Multi-tenant — each municipality's data is fully isolated
  • Role-based access — Auditor, Director, CFO, MM each see only what they need
  • MFMA workflow-aligned — finding stages mirror AGSA protocols
  • AI-assisted analysis — identifies trends, repeat findings, at-risk votes
  • Configurable branding — entity logo, name, leadership details per municipality
  • Offline-capable — field asset verification without internet dependency

Who Uses It

  • Internal Audit — captures and tracks every finding with evidence
  • City Manager / MM — assigns findings, monitors remediation progress
  • CFO — approves closures, signs Section 71 reports automatically
  • Directorate Managers — implements corrective actions with deadlines
  • Council / MPAC — oversight dashboard and escalation notifications
  • National Treasury / AGSA — structured evidence package at year-end

Why Now? The SALGA Opportunity

SALGA's mandate is to strengthen local government capacity. PSFGS directly advances three of SALGA's stated 2024–2029 strategic priorities: financial sustainability, good governance, and audit outcome improvement.

A SALGA-endorsed master agreement removes the need for each of the 257 municipalities to run individual procurement processes.
